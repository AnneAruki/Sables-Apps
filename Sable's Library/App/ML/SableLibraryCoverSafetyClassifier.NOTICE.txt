SableLibraryCoverSafetyClassifier

https://huggingface.co/deepghs/anime_rating

Sable's Library removed the original three-class output head and trained a
four-level ordinal safety head using user-reviewed manga and light-novel cover
artwork. The final output includes a conservative upward bias so uncertain
predictions favor a stricter warning.
